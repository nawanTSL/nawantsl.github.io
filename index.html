About

Senior Backend & Full-Stack Engineer · Technical Product Lead

My career started on the operations side — IT support, production monitoring, and incident troubleshooting. Those years taught me something that shaped everything I've done since: software doesn't end when you finish building it. It needs to work reliably when real people depend on it.

From there, I moved into backend development, working on settlement monitoring and bank reconciliation systems where precision and reliability weren't optional. Eventually, I started building my own products — learning platforms, document workflow systems, and mobile apps — and took on technical leadership, coordinating teams across backend, frontend, mobile, QA, and design.

Today, I lead a company and a team of around 12 people, but I stay close to the technical work. I'm comfortable discussing business requirements with stakeholders one moment and debugging a production issue the next. Having a working proficiency in English, I'm open to collaborating with clients across different time zones — schedules are always something we can work out.

I'm most useful when a project needs someone who understands the product, can work through the technical details, and stays involved until the system runs dependably in production.

Working Principles

Understand the real problem

Before writing any code, make sure the problem is clearly defined and the solution actually addresses what users need.

Make trade-offs visible

Every technical decision has consequences — surface them early so the team and stakeholders can decide together.

Build for maintainability

Code that works today but can't be understood or changed tomorrow isn't really done.

Stay accountable after release

Shipping is the halfway point. Monitor, respond, fix, and improve — that's where reliability is earned.
